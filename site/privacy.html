GPT Bulk

Privacy

Prompt and conversation data

GPT Bulk saves queued prompts and information about pending sends in browser extension storage on your device. It reads relevant parts of the ChatGPT page to verify prompt text, submission, and reply completion. It sends your queued text to ChatGPT through your signed-in browser session when you start the queue. OpenAI handles submitted data under its own privacy policy.

Access and retention

The extension runs on chatgpt.com and uses the browser’s storage permission to retain your queue. It does not send data to the GPT Bulk website, use telemetry, sell your data, or share it with advertisers. You can remove queued items in the panel or clear the extension’s local data by uninstalling it.

Questions

Use the project issue tracker. Avoid including private prompts in a public issue.
